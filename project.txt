Galaga game flow:
Loads controls and Stars -> Waiting
Waiting (Waiting for coin insert and pressing start) -> Startup
Startup (Displays level and loads player, if not already loaded) -> WaveStart
WaveStart (Loads wave of enemies) -> Playing
Playing (Main section, controls game flow and checks if players dies or enemies have been cleared) -> Respawn, GameOver, Startup, Winning
Respawn (Waits for enemies to stop moving and then respawns player) -> Playing
GameOver (Player has died, displays score) -> Waiting
Winning (player has won, displays score) -> Waiting
